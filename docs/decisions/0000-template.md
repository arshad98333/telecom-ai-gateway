# N. A short title, in the present tense, stating the decision

## Context

What was true when this came up, and what forced a choice. Facts, not narrative.

## Decision

What was decided, in one or two sentences, active voice.

## Alternatives considered

What else was on the table, and the specific reason each was not taken. "Rejected
because" — an alternative with no stated reason reads later as one nobody thought of.

## Consequences

What this makes easy, what it makes hard, and what now has to be true elsewhere.
Include the costs. A record that lists only benefits is advertising.
